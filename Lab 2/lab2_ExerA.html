About the Event

The Hackathon is a great opportunity to showcase your skills and collaborate with other students. It's a chance to learn new technologies, solve real-world problems, and have fun!

Programming Workshop

Students will learn basic programming techniques and practise solving problems using code.

Team Formation and Project Planning

Students will form teams and plan their projects, including defining goals, assigning tasks, and creating a timeline.
